# BUILD REQUEST: ASHLEY AMANI WAMBURA — PROFESSIONAL CHEF PORTFOLIO

Build a production-ready professional portfolio website for **Ashley Amani Wambura**, a Kenyan pastry and bakery professional.

The purpose of the website is to help Ashley present her professional experience, culinary skills, education, work history and food portfolio to potential employers in hotels, restaurants, bakeries, pastry kitchens, catering companies and hospitality organizations.

This is a professional chef portfolio — NOT a restaurant ordering website and NOT a developer portfolio.
